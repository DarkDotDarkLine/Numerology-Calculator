## 2. Information We Collect and How We Use It

When you access or use the application, certain information is collected automatically or provided voluntarily by you:

### A. Device Telemetry & Technical Information (Automatic)
To ensure app security, provide dynamic remote configuration (maintenance alerts, language settings, force update checks), and prevent misuse, our backend service (**Supabase**) automatically collects:
- **Device Identifiers:** A pseudonymous device identifier (`device_id` / Android ID)
- **Device Specifications:** Device manufacturer, model, and hardware details (e.g., Xiaomi 22041216I)
- **Operating System:** Android OS version (e.g., Android 14)
- **App Version:** Current installed app version code and name
- **Usage Metrics:** Session count and last active timestamp

*Purpose:* Managing remote configuration, preventing fraudulent activity, diagnostic monitoring, and serving app updates.

### B. User Feedback and Voluntary Communications
When you voluntarily use the in-app **Feedback & Suggestions** feature, we collect:
- Selected feedback category (e.g., Bug Report, Feature Suggestion, General Review)
- Your written message/feedback
- Your email address (**optional**, only if you provide it for a direct reply)
- Optional star rating score

*Purpose:* To resolve bugs, improve app features, and respond to your customer support inquiries.

The Numerology Calculator only requests the standard network permissions necessary for app functionality:
